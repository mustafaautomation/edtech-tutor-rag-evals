## Interpretation

The notes say a 95 percent confidence level is about the method, not a probability that the parameter sits inside one finished interval.

## Standard error

For a mean, the standard error in these notes is the sample standard deviation divided by the square root of the sample size.

## Interval form

The interval is the sample mean plus or minus a critical value times the standard error.
