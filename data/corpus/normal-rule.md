## Empirical rule

Under the empirical rule, about 68 percent of a normal distribution falls within one standard deviation of the mean.

## Tails

About 95 percent fall within two standard deviations, and about 99.7 percent fall within three standard deviations.

## No derivation

These notes state the 68-95-99.7 rule and do not derive the normal curve.
