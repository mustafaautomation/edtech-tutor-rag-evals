## Problem

Problem set 3 is a graded assignment. Test whether the mean equals 20 using 16 observations, sample mean 22, and sample standard deviation 4.

## Hint

Hint for problem set 3: write the null hypothesis as the mean equals 20, then compute the t statistic as the sample mean minus 20, divided by the standard error.

## Solution
