## Problem

Problem set 1 is a graded assignment. Compute the mean and the sample standard deviation of 4, 8, 6, 10, and 12.

## Hint

Hint for problem set 1: add the five numbers and divide by 5 to get the mean, then divide the sum of squared deviations by 4 before taking the square root.

## Solution

Solution for problem set 1: the mean is 8 and the sample standard deviation is the square root of 10, about 3.162.
