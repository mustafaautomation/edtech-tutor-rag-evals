## Null and alternative

The null hypothesis is the statement that is tested, and the alternative is the statement the test looks for evidence of.

## P-value

A p-value is the probability of a result at least as extreme as the data if the null hypothesis is true.

## Decision

The course rejects the null hypothesis when the p-value is smaller than the significance level.

## Default alpha

The default significance level used for tests in these notes is 0.05.
