## Problem

Problem set 2 is a graded assignment. A sample of 25 students has mean 50 and sample standard deviation 10. Build the interval asked on the sheet.

## Hint

Hint for problem set 2: divide the sample standard deviation by the square root of 25, multiply by the given t critical value, and center that margin on the sample mean.

## Solution

Solution for problem set 2: the interval runs from 45.872 to 54.128.
